# Eyeball Factory — Exec Ops

Working repo for the Executive Operations Assistant to **Shabir**, founder of Eyeball Factory.

## The three rules

1. **Nothing goes to a client without Shabir's OK.**
2. **Never commit a date or a rate on his behalf.**
3. **Check the version before you forward a file.**

These are absolute. They override speed, convenience, and a client waiting. See `01-guardrails.md`
for how each one is actually checked.

## Daily cadence

| When | What | To | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| by 2:20 PM | Start-of-day note — what I'm doing today, what I need from him | Shabir | WhatsApp |
| by 6:00 PM | End-of-day message — read on his phone, so short lines, no tables | Shabir | WhatsApp |

The end-of-day message always carries four things:
- what moved
- what needs him (numbered, so he can reply "1 yes, 2 no")
- **one thing I could take off his plate**, with a weekly time-saved estimate and the math behind it
- my Claude chat links for the day

## Ask when something is missing

Being blocked is not the failure. Going quiet about it is. Open questions live in
`04-open-questions.md` and the oldest ones get surfaced in the end-of-day note.

## Files

- `00-operating-brief.md` — the map: people, channels, time zones, how he works, where this is going
- `01-guardrails.md` — the three rules as working procedures, with ready-to-send language
- `02-system-map.md` — what already exists in Notion, what the gaps are, and what is next
- `03-note-templates.md` — start-of-day and end-of-day formats, and the time-saved method
- `04-open-questions.md` — what I need from you that I haven't got
- `daily/` — the sent notes, one file per day

**The live system is in Notion**, not here: Eyeball Factory OPS → Creatives → Core Pipeline for
the work, and Decisions & Blockers → Open Decisions for what is waiting on a person. This repo is
the working layer: how I do the job, not the state of the job. `02-system-map.md` has the full
picture.
