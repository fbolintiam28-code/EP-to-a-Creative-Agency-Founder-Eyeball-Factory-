# The daily cadence

For whoever runs this on Monday. Five minutes in the morning, five at the end. Do it in this order —
the order is the point, because each step feeds the next.

---

## Morning — 5 minutes

Open **Home → Decisions & Blockers**, then the two pipelines. Check in this order:

| # | Check | Where | Why it is this early |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Anything aged 3+ days with Shabir** | Open Decisions, sorted by Age | These go in today's note whatever else happens. His silence means he thinks it is handled, so age is the only thing that gets a decision made. |
| 2 | **Blocked = yes** | Core Pipeline | Someone is stuck right now. Everything else can wait five minutes; this cannot. |
| 3 | **Stale? = Stale** | Core Pipeline | 2+ days in the same status. Nobody reports these — the column is the only thing that notices. |
| 4 | **Owner is empty, or "Nobody"** | Both pipelines | A card with no owner is not slow, it is abandoned. This is the cheapest problem to fix and the most expensive to miss. |
| 5 | **Status = 5 Usability review** | Creator Deliveries | The step Shabir said "goes quiet". If anything has sat here overnight, it is already repeating the old pattern. |
| 6 | **Days Since Delivered > 7** | Creator Deliveries | Footage already paid for, doing nothing. |
| 7 | **Payment Status = Overdue** | Creator Deliveries | Fix it before a creator chases, because once they chase they chase Kayhan or Shabir. |
| 8 | **Waiting To Hear not empty** | Creators | Somebody is owed an answer from us. |

Then **run the Chaser** (`chaser/README.md`) on whatever came in overnight, send the chases it
drafts, and write the state changes back into Notion.

**Send the start-of-day note by 2:20 PM** (10:20 AM Dubai — his mid-morning).

---

## End of day — 5 minutes

1. **Write back** everything that moved today. Statuses, owners, new ETAs as estimates, new rows in
   Open Decisions.
2. **Tick `Creator Told`** on any creator delivery that reached an outcome today. A finished
   delivery with that box unticked is a creator sitting in silence.
3. **Send the end-of-day note by 6:00 PM** (2:00 PM Dubai — his early afternoon, so a question in
   it can still come back the same working day).

The note carries four things: what moved, what needs him (numbered), one thing you would take off
his plate with the weekly hours and the math, and the day's Claude chat links.

## What happens when something slips

### An owner misses a date

**First miss** — chase them once, in their channel, and ask for a new date in their own words.
Record it as *their estimate*. It is not a commitment and you never relay it as one. Shabir is not
involved. One person being slow once is not news.

**Second miss on the same item** — stop chasing. Three chases produce three apologies and no
output. Instead:
- Note what it blocks downstream — the batch, the client review, the live date. If nothing moves
  because of it, say that too; it may not have mattered.
- Put it to Shabir as a **choice**, not a complaint: the plan was wrong, the person is overloaded,
  or the work is harder than scoped. Those have different fixes and only he can pick.
- Phrase it so he can answer in a voice note.

There is no third miss, because a second one ends in a decision.

**Never name-and-shame.** "CB-10 has missed twice, here is what it blocks, here are the options"
— not "the strategist keeps missing deadlines." You are removing friction, not collecting evidence.

### Shabir does not reply

Re-ask. Every day, with the age stated. Never read silence as a yes — he goes quiet because he
assumes it is handled, so a non-reply is the opposite of approval.

If a client is waiting on something he has not answered, the client gets an acknowledgement that
commits to nothing. Buying a day honestly beats guessing his answer.

### A client asks for a date or a price

You do not have one. Say it will come from Shabir, flag it to him the same day. See
`01-guardrails.md` for the exact wording.

### Someone asks you to forward a file

Confirm the version with its owner first. Not with the thread, not with the folder — the person who
made it. If you cannot confirm, do not forward; ask. One question costs a minute. A client
reviewing a superseded cut costs Shabir a conversation he should not have to have.

### A creator delivers and nothing happens

That is the default failure on the creator side, not an exception. It is caught by checks 5 and 6
each morning. Anything in `5 Usability review` for more than a day gets a named owner that day,
even if the owner is you.

## The weekly beat

Still unscheduled, and it is the biggest single gap in the system. The weekly performance review is
what produces verdicts, learnings, and the next batch request that restarts the whole cycle. Without
a slot in the week it does not happen, so new batches start only when somebody remembers — which is
the exact thing this system exists to replace.

**Getting a day and time from Shabir is the highest-value thing on the open list.**

## If you only have one minute

Open Decisions, sorted by Age. Anything 3+ days old goes to Shabir today.

Everything else in this document is how you stop things reaching three days.
